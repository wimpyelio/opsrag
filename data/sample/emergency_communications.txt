TITLE: National Emergency Communications Plan
SOURCE: https://www.cisa.gov/national-emergency-communications-plan
TOPIC: emergency communications
SECTION: NECP goals
The National Emergency Communications Plan (NECP) is a strategic plan for strengthening emergency communications capabilities and interoperability. Its goals include governance and leadership, planning and procedures, training and exercises, communications coordination, technology and infrastructure, and cybersecurity. The plan is intended to support responders and partners who share information during incidents and planned events.
