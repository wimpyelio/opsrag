TITLE: FEMA EOC Operations
SOURCE: https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_eoc-references-resources-tool_2.pdf
TOPIC: emergency operations center
SECTION: Operations
EOC operations can help establish, maintain, and share situational awareness. The FEMA reference describes EOCs as helping coordinate information and resources, support long-term operations, improve continuity, provide access to essential information, and simplify information analysis and verification. Resource identification and assignment are also part of the coordination role.
