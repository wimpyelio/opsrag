TITLE: Initial Response Checklist
SOURCE: https://www.cisa.gov/safecom/planning-resources
TOPIC: emergency response
SECTION: Initial response
CISA provides an Initial Response Checklist for Statewide Interoperability Coordinators and related public safety deployments. The checklist recommends items to bring during an emergency response and covers scenarios such as basic Emergency Operations Center deployment, field or technical deployment, and Communications Unit deployment. A condensed version is also described for printing and carrying during response work.
